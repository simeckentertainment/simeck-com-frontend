About Us

Who We Are ✦

Born from a Phone Call

In 2013, Randy Simon and Carl Mineck had a wild idea: "Hey, wanna start an animation studio?" Within a year they'd packed up, teamed up, and launched Simeck Entertainment in Phoenix — the name a portmanteau of their last names, and a reminder that everything starts with friendship.

🎨 Where We Started

We began as an animation studio and followed our curiosity into comics, then games. The medium changes — the mission never does: create great art while treating people with compassion.

🎮 What We Make

Colorful, silly, and family-friendly — made for kids, with a wink for the parents. We want everyone to walk away smiling, feeling like they just had a genuinely fun time.

🤝 How We Work

We believe when you care for your people, your people thrive. No crunch, no exploitation — just good friends making art they're proud of.

🌟 Where We're Going

More games, more stories, more joy. Simeck is more than entertainment — it's play, kindness, and the belief that art can bring people together.
